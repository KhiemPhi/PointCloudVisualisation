Instructions:
    • Double-click on the plot to add a new point
    • The depth slider sets the z-coordinate
    • New points are labeled "Custom Point", appear in red, and have part_label -1
    • Click on a custom point to select it (only custom points – those with a "custom" flag – are modifiable)
    • Click "Delete Selected Point" to remove the selected custom point
    • Use the "Assign to Part" dropdown and click "Assign Selected Point" to reassign the custom point; its part_label is then updated (stored as an integer) while the "custom" flag persists
    • Click two points (loaded or custom) in sequence to draw an arrow between them
    • Select an object and batch from the dropdowns
    • Click on any point to add a directional arrow
    • Choose a direction (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z) from the popup menu
    • To change the arrow direction, click on another point
    • The arrow represents the axis direction for the screw
